# 1 — Plan

The lifecycle's first stage. Turns a vague ask into a written, unambiguous intent through genuine back-and-forth discussion — not a form to fill in once.

## Inputs

Whatever the user brings — an idea, a problem, a half-formed feature request. Nothing needs to exist yet.

## Skill

[[qsdlc-interview]] runs the interview: problem, target users, success criteria, constraints, explicit non-goals, prior art. It keeps going until things are actually resolved, recording anything genuinely unresolved under Intent.md's "Open questions" rather than guessing.

## Outputs

- **Document — `Intent.md`**: the full interpretation of what's being accomplished. See [[Documents]] for the exact structure.
- **Artifact — inspiration**: an evocative, visual representation of the vision — mood, tone, references. Deliberately *not* a spec; see [[Artifacts]].

## Hand-off

[[2 - Design]] reads `Intent.md` as its starting point. If Design finds Intent.md missing or unclear, it stops and sends work back here rather than inventing requirements.
